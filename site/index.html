Questions

Before you reserve

Do I pay anything to reserve?

No. Borrowed is in pre-launch. Reserving is free and non-binding — we collect no payment details. When rentals open in Spring 2027, reservation holders get first access and 20% off their first rental.

When do rentals start?

We plan to begin shipping in Spring 2027, in time for spring formals, career fairs and graduation season. We'll email you before your event to confirm details.

What if it doesn't fit?

Every rental ships with a backup size. If neither works, we'll swap it for a similar look or refund the rental in full.

Who is Borrowed for?

College students and recent graduates who want a standout look a few times a year — a gown for formal, a suit for the career fair, or a grail streetwear fit for a concert — without buying something they'll wear once.

How long can I keep it?

Standard rentals are four days. Extensions will be available at launch for a small daily fee.
