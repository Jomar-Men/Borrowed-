# Borrowed: launch Reel shot list (Higgsfield)

**Format:** 9:16 vertical, about 15 seconds total. Five clips of 2–3 seconds each, then the Borrowed end card (borrowed-endcard.mp4, 3.5s).
**Look:** an editorial photoshoot. A dark studio with a seamless backdrop, a hard key light and a camera flash popping. The grade is black-and-cream and slightly desaturated, with film grain.
**Cast:** two models, one man and one woman, both early 20s. Keep the same two models in every shot so it feels like one shoot.
**How:** for each shot, upload the product photo from the site's `images/` folder as the reference, then use image-to-video (or Higgsfield's fashion and product tools) with the prompt below.

---

### Shot 1 · Cold open (2s)
**Reference:** none
**Prompt:** Dark photo studio, seamless black backdrop, a photographer's flash pops and lights the frame for a moment, a clothing rack of designer pieces in soft focus, slow push-in, editorial fashion campaign, 35mm film grain, vertical 9:16.

### Shot 2 · Him, statement jacket (3s)
**Reference:** `navy-velvet-dinner-jacket.jpg` (or burgundy)
**Prompt:** A young male model in a navy velvet dinner jacket over a black shirt, adjusting his cuff, a camera flash fires twice, he turns his head toward the lens, hard studio key light, black seamless backdrop, editorial menswear campaign, slow orbit, film grain.

### Shot 3 · Her, streetwear (3s)
**Reference:** `chrome-hearts-cross-patch-jeans.jpg` plus `chrome-hearts-paper-chain-necklace.jpg`
**Prompt:** A young female model in a cropped black tee, leather cross-patch jeans and a heavy sterling silver chain necklace, a confident walk toward the camera, flashes popping, low angle, black studio backdrop, luxury streetwear editorial, film grain.

### Shot 4 · Detail montage (3s)
**Reference:** `chrome-hearts-spacer-0.jpg`, `chrome-hearts-cemetery-cross-belt.jpg`
**Prompt:** Extreme close-ups in quick cuts: a hand with stacked sterling silver rings, a silver cross belt buckle being fastened, a luxury steel wristwatch catching the light, macro lens, hard flash, black background, high-end jewelry campaign.

### Shot 5 · Both together (3s)
**Reference:** shot 2 and shot 3 outfits
**Prompt:** The same two models side by side, he in the velvet dinner jacket and she in the streetwear look, holding a final pose as several camera flashes go off, then they look straight into the lens, black seamless studio, editorial campaign finale, slow pull-back, film grain.

### Shot 6 · End card (3.5s)
Use **borrowed-endcard.mp4** as is.
